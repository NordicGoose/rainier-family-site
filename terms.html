Terms of use

Rainier Family is a private assistant available by invitation to authorized members of one household. It helps with everyday information and summaries from services that adult members choose to connect.

Your account and permissions

Connect only an account you own or are authorized to use. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account. The initial Google connection reads information; it does not send or delete Gmail messages or change Google content. Any future Google write feature requires a separate explanation and consent.

Using the assistant

Summaries can be incomplete or mistaken. Check important details in the original source before relying on them. Access to connected services depends on your permissions and the availability of those services.

Privacy and questions

The privacy policy explains how connected Google information is used, shared within the household, retained, and removed. For questions, email contact@rainier.app.
