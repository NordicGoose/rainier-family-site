Use the Rainier app already installed on your device, or continue to the private web app. Sign-in and household access happen there.

Continue to private Rainier

Connect your enrolled device to the household's Tailscale network first. Rainier will then use its existing device setup and passkey sign-in.

Open private Rainier ↗

This opens a separate private address. This public page doesn't collect account details, device tokens, or household information.

The private app won't open

Check that Tailscale is connected on this device and that your household invitation is complete. A private address may be unreachable from an unconnected device. Contact your household administrator if access still fails.

I'm using a new device

Ask your household administrator to enroll it using Rainier's existing setup flow. Enter any one-time setup code only in the private Rainier app. Keep setup codes and device tokens out of email and public pages.

I'm not a household member

Rainier is invite-only. This website doesn't create household accounts or provide public access to the private app. For general app or privacy questions, email contact@rainier.app.
